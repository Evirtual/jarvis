# Supporting the console

The console is free, open source (AGPL-3.0) and runs on your own keys, so
it costs nothing to run for anyone else. Two ways to say thanks, both
optional:

- **GitHub Sponsors**: <https://github.com/sponsors/Evirtual>, one-off or
  monthly, by card, no fees taken from individual developers.
- **Buy Me a Coffee**: a coffee, one-off or monthly. (Link added once the
  page exists.)

Nothing is locked behind a donation, and nothing is promised for one. The
tiers are a thank-you, not a contract.

## The tiers, as pasted into GitHub Sponsors

Enable one-time payments as well as monthly ones. Three tiers is enough;
more only makes people hesitate.

**Coffee · $3 a month (or one-off)**
> A coffee for the butler. Thank you, sir — or ma'am.

**Supporter · $10 a month**
> Your name in the README's supporters list, if you'd like it there, and my
> genuine thanks. This keeps the free tier free: no ads, no accounts, no
> keys anywhere but your own device.

**Patron · $25 a month**
> Everything above, and a say in what comes next: patrons get an early look
> at what's being built and are asked first which of it matters.

Sponsors-page bio (the short text at the top):

> J.A.R.V.I.S. Console is an Iron Man–style console that talks back: real
> machine and network readings, a voice, and threads of research you run by
> talking. It runs on your own API keys and keeps them on your device. It's
> free and open source; sponsoring keeps it that way.

## Setting the accounts up (once, by the owner)

**GitHub Sponsors**

1. github.com → your profile photo → **Your sponsors** (or
   <https://github.com/sponsors/accounts>) → **Join** for the personal
   account.
2. Fill in the profile: the bio above, and the three tiers. Tick "one-time".
3. **Payout**: GitHub connects you to Stripe; it asks for a bank account and
   an identity check. This is the receiving side and has nothing to do with
   the card you pay GitHub with.
4. Tax form (W-8BEN for non-US residents; it's short).
5. Submit. Approval takes a few days; the **Sponsor** button on the
   repository appears by itself once it's through, because
   `.github/FUNDING.yml` already names the account.

**Buy Me a Coffee**

1. buymeacoffee.com → sign up, pick a page name (short: `evirtual` or
   `jarvisconsole`).
2. Connect the payout (Stripe or PayPal, into the same bank account).
3. Set the "coffee" price to $3 and paste the same bio.
4. Send the page name here: it goes in `.github/FUNDING.yml`, the README
   and the app's Configuration drawer.

## Where the links live

- The repository's **Sponsor** button (`.github/FUNDING.yml`).
- The README's *Support* section.
- The app: one line at the bottom of Configuration → Quick.
